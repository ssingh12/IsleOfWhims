Isle of Whims

Each player is a minor god with a secret fate in mind. The creatures think for themselves. All you can do is nudge the island around them.

Deal the fates passes this screen round. Play with phones turns each friend's phone into their own controller. No accounts or sign-in needed.

How a round goes

  1. Pass the phone round. Each god secretly sees one fate.
  2. The island wakes. Anyone can spend their 60 tokens on nudges, at any time.
  3. The first fate to come true ends the round. Its god wins.
